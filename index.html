You debate every week

Students improve by speaking, not by watching. Every session ends in a round, and nobody sits out.

Every round gets a ballot

You get the same written decision a tournament judge would give you — what won, what lost, and the one thing to fix next.

We teach argument, not scripts

Memorized cases fall apart under cross-examination. Students learn to build and defend their own reasoning.

Coaches who still judge

Our staff judge 40+ rounds a season, so the advice reflects how rounds are actually being decided this year.

Small on purpose

Eight students means every case gets read, edited, and re-read by a coach — not skimmed.

Skills that outlast the season

Research, writing, and speaking under pressure show up in class presentations, interviews, and applications long after the trophies.
